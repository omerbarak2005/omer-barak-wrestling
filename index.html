Omer Barak is a member of the University of Pennsylvania wrestling team who has represented Israel in freestyle and Greco-Roman competition.

He won senior freestyle gold at 86 kg at the 2024 Pat Shaw Memorial in Guatemala. That year, he also represented Israel at the U20 European Championships.

His background began in judo. Before Penn, he trained and studied at Lake Highland Prep in Florida.
