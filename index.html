Omer Barak: from Florida’s wrestling mats to representing Israel

The Penn wrestler began in judo, moved away from home to train, and went on to win international medals for Israel.

In June 2024, Omer Barak won gold for Israel at the Pat Shaw Memorial in Guatemala City, finishing first in senior men’s freestyle at 86 kilograms. USA Wrestling included his result in its report on the tournament.

The result followed a change of sports and a move away from home. A national champion in judo, Barak took up wrestling in high school when he realized he needed to keep improving as an athlete. To attend the wrestling program at Lake Highland Prep in Florida, he moved away from his parents. A March 2024 feature in the school newspaper described how his judo background influenced his wrestling style.

Barak credited coach Mike Palazzo and the team’s culture with helping him improve. The school feature described the less visible demands of competing: travel, homework at the airport and returning to school after late flights. As Barak put it in that interview, “the hard work you put in shows. There’s no hiding.”

He went on to join the University of Pennsylvania’s wrestling roster. Penn’s official biography lists a high school record of 223 wins and 38 losses, five All-American honors and two national finals appearances. Those honors belong to his high school career.

His international competition record developed alongside his American training. In 2024, Barak represented Israel at the U20 European Championships in both freestyle and Greco-Roman wrestling. He returned to the Pat Shaw Memorial in 2025, where United World Wrestling records senior gold medals at 87 kilograms in Greco-Roman and 92 kilograms in freestyle.

Barak is now listed on Penn’s wrestling roster. His university profile and international results document the two settings in which he has built his wrestling career: an American collegiate program and international tournaments representing Israel.
