Omer Barak: from judo to international wrestling

Omer Barak, a member of the University of Pennsylvania wrestling team, has represented Israel in both freestyle and Greco-Roman competition. In June 2024, he won senior freestyle gold at 86 kg at the Pat Shaw Memorial in Guatemala. USA Wrestling recorded the result in its tournament coverage.

Barak returned to the Pat Shaw Memorial in 2025. United World Wrestling records gold medals in senior Greco-Roman at 87 kg and senior freestyle at 92 kg. His international experience also includes representing Israel at the 2024 U20 European Championships in both styles. View the UWW record.

Before Penn, Barak trained and studied at Lake Highland Prep in Florida. A March 2024 school newspaper feature described his background in judo and his move away from his parents to attend the wrestling program. He credited coach Mike Palazzo and the team’s culture with helping him improve. Read the school newspaper feature.

Penn’s athlete biography lists a high school record of 223 wins and 38 losses, five All-American honors and two national finals appearances. These distinctions refer to his high school career. View his Penn profile.
